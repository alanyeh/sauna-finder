KORIBOSHI WHOLESALE & PARTNERSHIP PROSPECTS
Research date: October 5, 2026

SCOPE
Read the current Sauna Finder Supabase saunas table without changing it: 211 listings. All candidates originate in that table. Websites and targeted searches enriched existing businesses; no unrelated new businesses were added. Customer/order files were not used. No outreach was sent, forms submitted, or database/app changes made.

The 211 listings map to 161 research accounts: Top 20, Next 30, and 111 long-tail accounts. See database-to-prospect-audit.csv for every original row and its assigned account. Same-brand locations are consolidated; no assumption is made that individual sites buy independently. Domain grouping was manually adjusted for Bay Club/Magnuson. Sauna Hut and Steamworks same-name locations are provisionally combined to avoid duplicate outreach; common ownership/buying authority across those domains remains unverified. Distinct businesses with the name Loyly are kept separate. Current public sites may list more locations than the database; upcoming sites are not counted as confirmed operating sites.

RANKING
The top 50 are individually reviewed and editorially ranked by the practical chance of a useful first relationship: sauna centrality, repeat community, product/retail relevance, design/hospitality alignment, operator scale, contactability, supplier conflict and timing. This is not a ranking by followers or business size. Scores support that judgment rather than mechanically determine the order. A low-volume sampling relationship can outrank a larger possible retailer.

Wholesale potential: 1 = weak category/channel fit; 2 = possible but peripheral; 3 = credible small trial with retail uncertainty; 4 = strong sauna/customer fit and plausible retail or gifting channel; 5 = unusually direct product, retail or existing-hat-use evidence. Scores are relative opportunity estimates, not confirmed demand or conversion probabilities.
Relationship potential: 1 = little relevant community/accessible local connection; 2 = general wellness overlap; 3 = relevant but less distinct or less verified; 4 = strong sauna culture, guest or program alignment; 5 = unusually close operator/community fit for samples, feedback or collaboration.
Decision-maker access: 1 = centralized chain/unclear route; 2 = weak or indirect route; 3 = verified general business route but buyer unnamed; 4 = named owner/founder plus business contact, or explicit partnership form; 5 = published named partnership contact. A founder name plus a shared mailbox is not a verified direct founder email.
High = top 20; Medium = next 30; Low = long tail for this campaign. Long-tail ordering is approximate triage (45% relationship, 35% wholesale, 20% access), not the same depth of qualification as the top 50.

EVIDENCE AND UNKNOWNS
Official websites were primary sources. Public founder interviews, a design agency portfolio and tourism listings supplied some names or corroboration. Each prospect includes source URLs and an email source. A source list may support several fields; editorial fit, scores and proposed outreach are explicitly analyst judgments. Exact city/state/country comes from database street addresses, with current-site corrections where found; a database metro tag is not treated as the physical city.

Published emails were not guessed, sent to, or deliverability-tested. “Not found” and “Not verified” mean no reliable public value was established in this review. Many named contacts are reached through a general inbox. Roles marked “requested role” describe whom to ask for, not a verified employee. All prospects remain Not contacted; prior relationships or outreach outside this session are unknown, so reconcile with your own history before sending.

Physical retail is distinguished from gift cards, included amenities, rentals, and design mockups. A shop link alone does not prove stock or on-site sales. Existing hat sales are both category validation and a possible supplier conflict. Most businesses did not publish purchasing budgets, minimum orders or willingness to stock another brand. “1 venue shown” is a lower-bound observation, not proof there are no other locations.

Instagram links were taken from official websites or explicit official references where available. Follower counts, recent posting frequency and engagement were not reliably verified and are not used as a scoring shortcut. A linked profile is not proof of a strong social presence. Some profiles are brand-wide rather than location-specific; these are noted. Website retrieval failures are not labeled as closure. Long-tail retail, recurring programs and contact people may remain unqualified even when a contact-page scan succeeded.

CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC FINDINGS
Start with Loyly Portland for a retail trial, Soho for feedback on its existing guest-hat use, Element for member gifting, SaunaGlo for an owner relationship, and Red Rock for a small merchandise test.
Akari and Othership already sell sauna hats; Kolm's design agency shows branded hats. Acknowledge that and test interest in a cotton alternative rather than suggesting the category is missing.
NAOSU is an exact style benchmark. Its current terms name ERY Holdings LLC; approach the current team instead of assuming a historical founder relationship.
Fjord advertises construction-related irregular bookings through November; time a retail proposal around normal operations. Alchemy's Sauna Garden is operating, while its full bathhouse is still advertised as opening soon.
Toronto Sauna Festival's listed October 2–4, 2026 event has ended. It is a future-event relationship, not an imminent sampling slot.
Koti and Uptown are public-facing but home/backyard-scale operators: appealing personal relationships, deprioritized for residential setting and limited retail volume. Generic gyms, large infrared franchises and treatment-first businesses remain low priority.
Saint's working website is www.saint.nyc; Von's working website is www.vonsauna.com. Those findings are recorded here without editing the production database.

OUTREACH ASSUMPTIONS
Recommend asking whether they would like a sample before shipping it. Discuss wholesale terms only after fit is established. Custom/co-branded hats are exploratory proposals; production capability, minimums, pricing, lead time and Canadian landed costs have not been assumed or promised. Product-positioning assumptions follow koriboshi's public description of its Japanese-inspired, machine-washable cotton-blend hat (https://koriboshi.com/products/slate-grey), not a 100% cotton or medical-safety claim.
